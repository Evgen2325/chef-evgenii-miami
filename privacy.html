Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Chef Evgenii Miami handles information received through this website, Instagram, and the Chef Evgenii Instagram Agent.

Plain-language summary:

We use information only to answer inquiries, provide private-chef services, manage requested communications, and operate our Instagram account. We do not sell personal information.

Information we may receive

  • Contact details that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and preferred contact method.
  • Service details, including household size, general location, dietary preferences, allergies, scheduling needs, and information included in an inquiry.
  • Instagram interactions, including comments, direct messages, Instagram user identifiers, and the content needed to respond to those interactions.
  • Basic technical information that may be recorded by website or infrastructure providers for security and reliable operation.

How we use information

  • To respond to questions and consultation requests.
  • To prepare quotes, menus, schedules, and service arrangements.
  • To publish approved content and manage comments or messages on the Chef Evgenii Miami Instagram account.
  • To protect the service from misuse and diagnose technical problems.

Automated and AI-assisted replies

Some common Instagram questions may receive a prepared response. When AI-assisted replies are enabled, the text of a message may be processed by an AI service solely to draft a relevant business response. Sensitive dietary, medical, payment, or identity information should not be sent through Instagram messages.

Service providers

Information may be processed by providers that are necessary to operate the service, including Meta and Instagram, website or server infrastructure providers, and an AI provider when AI-assisted replies are enabled. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Storage and security

Access credentials are stored separately from public website files. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to restrict access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer an inquiry, provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal obligations. Instagram content and messages may also remain subject to Meta's retention practices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the service, when you request or authorize it, or when required by law.

Data access and deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information held by Chef Evgenii Miami. Send the request from the email address associated with your inquiry to evgenii31599@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” Include your Instagram username if the request concerns Instagram data. We may request reasonable verification before acting on the request.

Children

The service is intended for adults arranging private-chef or meal services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
